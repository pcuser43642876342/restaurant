our story

Rooted in tradition,
served with heart

What began as a small neighbourhood trattoria has grown into one of the city's most loved dining rooms. At Nomania Hotel we cook with the seasons — sourcing from farmers, fishers and makers we know by name.

Every plate is made from scratch: pasta rolled each morning, bread baked in-house, and a wine list that celebrates small, natural producers.
